NEED A QUICK ANSWER?

Frequently asked
questions.

Visit official AdZU website ↗
Where can I find my classroom?

Check your official class schedule and ask your department or campus personnel to confirm the room location.

Where can I confirm enrollment requirements?

Use the latest official enrollment announcement or contact the university office responsible for admissions and enrollment.

How do I complete my clearance?

Check the current clearance instructions and confirm all required sign-offs with the relevant offices.

How can I find student organizations?

Look for official university or student affairs announcements and verify each organization's current membership process.

Does ADZU BUDDY process payments or enrollment?

No. This project is an information guide only. It does not process enrollment, registration, payments, or official clearances.
